## Context

MoonBOT is QuickPower's decision-support platform for energy trading. Details of data sources, signals and models are confidential, so this page describes the platform at concept level.

## Platform

- **Live market view**: dashboards that follow the market as it moves, with trends, positions and exports.
- **Early warning**: continuous monitoring that flags unusual changes and notifies the team right away.
- **Forecasting**: a machine-learning ensemble that estimates where the market is heading over the next intervals.
- **Client reporting**: reports generated and refreshed automatically for each client.
- **Roles**: separate areas for administrators, traders and clients.

## Delivery

QA and production run as separate containerised stacks. Every change is checked and deployed to QA automatically; production releases are tagged and promoted through the same pipeline.
