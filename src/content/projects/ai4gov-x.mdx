## Context

AI4Gov-X is a four-year European project, co-funded by the Digital Europe Programme and coordinated by POLI.design, with more than 40 partner organisations in 21 countries. It builds the skills public administrations need to use AI responsibly, across three domains: digital governance and transformation, AI and data, and human-centred design and innovation. Its flagship is MAGIX, a fully online Specialising Master's (60 ECTS) led by Politecnico di Milano.

POLITEHNICA Bucharest runs the Romanian hub, which presents the project, its courses and activities to a Romanian audience.
