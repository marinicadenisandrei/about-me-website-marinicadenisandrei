## Context

DeCiTeL (Democratic Citizenship Teaching & Learning) is an Erasmus+ Teacher Academy (project no. 101196495), co-funded by the European Union. The platform is its digital home: a public site plus a member area for educators, teacher educators, researchers and institutions.

## Platform

- **Learning resource repository**: search, versions and file storage through pre-signed S3 URLs; authors submit resources for review and moderators publish them.
- **Communities of practice**: membership, profiles and partner suggestions.
- **Virtual exchanges**: the full lifecycle from proposal and expressions of interest to partners, delivery and completion.
- **Roles**: educators, teacher educators, researchers, institutions, moderators and administrators.

## Delivery

- **Environments**: QA on every push to `main`, production on `RELEASE` commits, both on self-hosted servers reached over Tailscale SSH from GitHub Actions.
- **Public access**: production runs behind a Cloudflare Tunnel whose routing, DNS, Access policies and SSL settings live in Terraform; QA uses a Tailscale Funnel.
- **Operations**: database migrations applied on container start, a health check every three hours with Slack alerts, and backups of the PostgreSQL and object-storage volumes.
