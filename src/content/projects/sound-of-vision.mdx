## System

- **Core hardware** — NVIDIA Jetson Xavier NX for AI tasks, an ESP32 master controller driving the audio headset and the haptic belt, and a Mini ESP32-C3 in the belt handling wireless charging and ESP-NOW command transmission.
- **Detection** — neural networks classify obstacles, text, pedestrians, sidewalks, stairs, doors and potholes.
- **Spatial audio** — positional feedback relative to the user, with effects tuned for object classification and navigation.
- **Haptics** — a fully wireless belt with control algorithms mapping detections to haptic parameters.

## Edge platform

A three-node NVIDIA Jetson Xavier NX cluster, fully containerised and orchestrated with Kubernetes, for real-time video analytics:

- CNN video pipelines in TensorFlow/Keras, optimised with CUDA, cuDNN and TensorRT; models served with NVIDIA Triton Inference Server; GStreamer and FFmpeg for pre- and post-processing.
- Infrastructure as code with Ansible and Chef; repeatable deployments with Helm, Kustomize, GitLab CI/CD and Argo CD; Kubeflow Pipelines and MLflow for MLOps.
- Apache Kafka and MQTT for video and telemetry streams; Ceph/MinIO object storage and NFS/GlusterFS shared volumes for datasets.
- Prometheus, Grafana, ELK and Jaeger tracing; Istio and Vault for service-to-service security; pytest unit/integration tests and Selenium end-to-end dashboard tests.
