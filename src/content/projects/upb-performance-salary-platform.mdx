## Context

POLITEHNICA Bucharest rewards the performance of its teaching and research staff with salary increases based on a set of criteria approved by the university's Administrative Board. The platform turns that policy into one online process, from the application to the approved, centralised list sent on for payment.

## How it works

1. **Apply.** Eligible staff sign in with their institutional account, choose the criteria they claim and upload the supporting documents. Results already known to the university are offered from reference lists instead of being typed in again.
2. **Verify.** The platform runs automatic checks first; reviewers then handle what needs a human eye and can ask for clarifications or reopen specific sections.
3. **Approve.** Complete, eligible applications move through the university's approval circuit, and the platform produces the centralised list of approved increases, per person and per criterion.
