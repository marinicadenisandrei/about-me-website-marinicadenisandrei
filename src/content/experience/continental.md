Continental's Autonomous Mobility business develops intelligent camera, lidar and radar sensors that give drivers active safety support: Emergency Brake Assist, Adaptive Cruise Control, Lane Keeping Support, Traffic Sign Recognition, Lane Departure Warning, Pedestrian Detection and Parking Assistance. The System Engineering team defines, designs and integrates these systems to customer requirements and project timing.

**Requirements engineering.** Review of customer and stakeholder requirements; allocation to individual system components and hand-over to the dedicated design teams; creation of requirements for those teams based on the system architecture.

**System architecture.** Concept definition and system design; integration of the safety concept; decomposition into components and disciplines (hardware, software, mechanical design); participation in discipline-architecture reviews and releases; periodic checks and updates of the system bill of materials.

**System FMEA.** Owner of the system FMEA with the support of an FMEA moderator; tracking of the implementation of the resulting actions.

**Domains touched.** HW/SW, QA, cybersecurity, functional safety, production and sensor technology. Alongside the architecture work: automated testing and deployment through dev-to-production environments, Linux (Red Hat) server administration and patching, VMware virtualisation, and scripting in Python and PowerShell.
