Automation engineers research, design and develop applications and systems for manufacturing-process automation, reducing human intervention where appropriate to reach the full potential of industrial robotics.

- Direct programming of Kawasaki industrial robots in Kawasaki K-ROSET and with the Teach Pendant interface; direct programming of collaborative Universal Robots arms.
- Design, automation, installation and integration of industrial robots and robotic cells; maintenance and installation visits at different clients.
- Collection and preparation of control-system documentation (PLCs, PCs and SCADA servers, I/O devices, instruments, variable-frequency drives, network components) and of automation software backups.
- Technical specifications for requests for quotation in electrical engineering and automation; supplier support during site visits and scope definition.
- Participation in design, factory acceptance tests, installation supervision, commissioning and final acceptance of instrumentation, control-system and MCC modernisation projects.
- Network and architecture definitions for IT/OT segregation, OT wireless and communication protocols (Profibus, OPC UA/DA, Profinet, UART, I²C, CAN), and integration with process historians.
