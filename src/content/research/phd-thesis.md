Expected completion: July 2028. Field of study: Information and Communication Technologies.

The thesis continues the development of the detection algorithms, spatial audio rendering, wireless haptic feedback and GPU-accelerated edge infrastructure started in the European projects **Sound of Vision** (real-time detection with audio-haptic feedback for the visually impaired) and **TRAVEE** (VR with augmented feedback for post-stroke neuromotor rehabilitation).

- **Detection algorithms** — obstacles, text, pedestrians, sidewalks and elements of interest (stairs, doors, potholes) with neural networks and deep-learning computer vision.
- **Spatial audio** — accurate positional feedback relative to the user, with audio effects optimised for object classification and navigation by sound.
- **Fully wireless haptic belt** — wireless charging and ESP-NOW communication, with control algorithms mapping detection output to haptic parameters.
- **Edge platform** — a three-node NVIDIA Jetson Xavier NX Kubernetes cluster running TensorFlow/Keras inference optimised with CUDA, cuDNN and TensorRT, served by NVIDIA Triton, with GStreamer/FFmpeg pre- and post-processing and MLOps tooling (Kubeflow Pipelines, MLflow, GitLab CI/CD, Argo CD).
